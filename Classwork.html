Top Tourist Destinations

1. Agra

State: Uttar Pradesh

Agra is famous for the magnificent Taj Mahal and is an important historical destination in India.

Interesting Fact:The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the Modern World.


2. Jaipur

State: Rajasthan

Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is renowned for its majestic palaces, forts, and vibrant culture.

Interesting Fact: Jaipur is part of the Golden Triangle tourist circuit, along with Delhi and Agra.


3. Goa

State: Goa

Goa is famous for its stunning beaches, vibrant nightlife, and Portuguese heritage.

Interesting Fact: Goa was a Portuguese colony for over 450 years, which has influenced its architecture, cuisine, and culture.


4. Manali

State: Himachal Pradesh

Manali is a popular hill station known for its scenic beauty, adventure sports, and serene landscapes.

Interesting Fact: Manali is a gateway to the Solang Valley, which is famous for skiing and paragliding.
